# Night Game Operations Brief — Sample DeskCast Source

## Opening situation
The road crew arrives at a high-visibility night event with compressed timelines,
elevated compliance checks, and weather risk after 21:00 local. Leadership wants
clarity on what actually matters in the first ninety minutes.

## Loadout and documentation
Primary packets include the master contract addendum, detention policy excerpts,
and the emergency contact matrix. Secondary packets cover lot diagrams and the
after-hours fuel plan. Anything not in the packet is treated as rumor until verified.

## Critical timeline
T-minus 120 minutes: final document audit and radio check.
T-minus 60 minutes: gate assignment confirmation and staging lane walk.
T-minus 15 minutes: all-call readiness and exception log review.
Post-event: chain-of-custody notes and detention timestamps filed the same night.

## Risk board
Weather crosswinds on the elevated approach, lot congestion near exit C, and
document mismatches on detention language are the three items most likely to
create cascading delay. Color commentary should hammer verification before
escalation.

## Call to action
Run the sniff-test on every signature block, confirm the detention clock start
rule in writing, and keep the desk loop open until the last unit clears.
