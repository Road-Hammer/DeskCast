# STWL Sample Technology Continuity Addendum (Demo RC1.0)

**Parties:** Susquehanna Timberwolf Lines, LLC ("Contractor") and Example Organization, LLC ("Organization").  
**Purpose:** Demo instrument for DeskCast legal structure + episode planner.  
**Disclaimer:** Sample text only. Not a real agreement.

## ARTICLE 0 - CONTROLLING AGREEMENT AND ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

0.1 Controlling Effect. This Addendum controls every subject it addresses. The Original Agreement remains effective only to the extent it is not changed or contradicted by this Addendum.

0.2 Order of Precedence. In the event of a conflict, the following order controls: (a) a later signed amendment that expressly modifies this Addendum; (b) this Addendum; (c) its Schedules and Appendices; (d) approved invoices and project-specific written terms accepted by Contractor; (e) the Original Agreement.

0.3 No Silent Waiver. Failure to enforce a provision shall not waive that provision or any other right under this Addendum.

## ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS

1.1 Software means the Hawk/WolfTMS systems, Road To Home modules, Fortress components, and related source, configurations, and documentation identified in Schedule G.

1.2 Specialized Services means work outside ordinary monthly support, including custom development, migrations, incident response beyond contracted hours, and Special Projects authorized in writing.

1.3 Plan B Option means Contractor's business-continuity, redundancy, and fallback operating capability for critical systems described in Schedule H.

1.4 Board Authorization means a written resolution or minutes entry of the Organization's governing body approving a Special Project, budget ceiling, or entity adoption action.

## ARTICLE 2 - COMPENSATION, PAYMENT, AND COST RECOVERY

2.1 Monthly Technology Retainer. Organization shall pay Contractor a monthly retainer of five thousand dollars ($5,000) on the first business day of each month for baseline support and continuity coverage.

2.2 Specialized Services Rates. Specialized Services shall be invoiced at Contractor's then-current rates, not less than one hundred seventy-five dollars ($175) per hour, unless a fixed-price statement of work is accepted in writing.

2.3 Nonpayment. If any undisputed invoice remains unpaid more than fifteen (15) days after the due date, Contractor may suspend non-critical services after three (3) days' written notice. Suspension shall not waive fees already earned.

2.4 Expenses. Pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses shall be reimbursed within thirty (30) days of invoice with receipts. Contractor shall not procure equipment above two thousand dollars ($2,000) without written approval except for emergency restoration under Article 14.

## ARTICLE 3 - AUTHORITY AND RELIANCE

3.1 Designated Contacts. Organization shall maintain at least one Designated Representative authorized to approve routine work tickets and one Board-level contact for Special Projects.

3.2 Reliance. Contractor may rely on written instructions from a Designated Representative. Instructions that expand scope, create Special Projects, or commit Organization funds above Schedule J thresholds require Board Authorization.

3.3 No Apparent Authority Trap. Silence or informal chat messages shall not create Board Authorization or a license under Article 7.

## ARTICLE 4 - PRIVATE PROPERTY AND FACILITY PROTECTION

4.1 Contractor Facility. Contractor systems hosted at Contractor premises remain Contractor property. Organization personnel shall not enter Contractor facilities without prior written consent and escort.

4.2 Organization Site Access. When Organization grants site access, it shall provide safe working conditions, electrical capacity information, and any site rules in writing before work begins.

4.3 Homestead / Sensitive Locations. Access to homestead or residential-adjacent sites is governed by Schedule A. Contractor may refuse unsafe conditions without breach.

## ARTICLE 7 - LICENSING

7.1 General Rule. Except for the limited internal-use license in Section 7.2, no license to Contractor intellectual property is granted by disclosure, integration, or payment of the monthly retainer alone.

7.2 Limited Internal-Use License. For fully paid periods, Organization receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable, internal-use license to operate production instances of Software solely for Organization's own operations, subject to this Addendum.

7.3 Restrictions. Organization shall not sublicense, reverse engineer, publicly publish, train public models on, or migrate Software to a third-party host without Contractor's prior written consent.

7.4 Termination of License. Upon material breach or nonpayment past thirty (30) days after notice, the Section 7.2 license may be suspended until cure.

## ARTICLE 9 - COUNCIL

9.1 Concept Only. "Council" is an AI-support concept and coordination label. Council is not a legal entity and does not hold property, contracts, or authority independent of the Parties.

9.2 No Automatic Agency. Outputs labeled Council recommendations are advisory tools. They do not bind either Party unless a human Designated Representative or Board Authorization adopts them in writing.

## ARTICLE 10 - AIR GAP PRINCIPLE

10.1 Separation. Contractor systems and Organization systems shall remain operationally separable. Shared credentials shall not be the sole recovery path for either Party.

10.2 No Forced Merger. Organization shall not require Contractor to permanently merge identity providers, root keys, or source repositories into Organization-controlled tenancy as a condition of payment for baseline services.

## ARTICLE 14 - BACKUPS, CREDENTIALS, AND RECORD RECOVERY

14.1 Backups. Contractor shall maintain backups of Contractor-controlled production configurations on a cadence described in Schedule H. Organization remains responsible for its own business data exports unless a Special Project states otherwise.

14.2 Credential Registry. Production credentials for jointly used services shall be inventoried in Schedule H. Emergency break-glass procedures require dual control where practical.

14.3 Recovery Priority. In a declared incident, life safety and data integrity come first; feature work is secondary. Contractor may implement protective measures without prior approval when delay would cause material harm, then notify Organization within forty-eight (48) hours.

## ARTICLE 25 - ORGANIZATIONAL RECORDS AND MINUTES

25.1 Minutes. Organization should maintain minutes of Board actions affecting technology authority, budgets, and Special Projects.

25.2 Contractor Copies. Contractor may keep copies of Board Authorizations that justify Specialized Services. Those copies remain confidential under the Parties' NDA.

## ARTICLE 32 - SPECIAL PROJECTS AND INVOICING

32.1 Definition. Special Projects are discrete efforts with a written scope, budget ceiling, and acceptance criteria, authorized under Schedule N.

32.2 Authorization. No Special Project is binding until Board Authorization (or delegated authority under Schedule J) and Contractor's written acceptance.

32.3 Scope Control. Revisions, additions, or feature expansions require a change order or updated authorization. Verbal "while you are there" work is not authorized above four (4) hours without follow-up written confirmation within five (5) business days.

32.5 Invoice Identification. Every Special Project invoice shall use a unique project code matching the authorization record.

## ARTICLE 35 - DATA, SECURITY, AND RESPONSIBLE USE

35.1 Responsible Use. Parties shall not use shared systems for unlawful surveillance, harassment, or unauthorized access to third-party systems.

35.2 Security Incidents. Suspected compromise of jointly used credentials shall be reported to the other Party promptly, not later than twenty-four (24) hours after discovery.

35.3 Model Training. Organization shall not feed Contractor confidential materials into public AI systems without written consent.

## SCHEDULE A - HOMESTEAD ACCESS AND OPERATIONAL SECURITY

A.1 Access Windows. Site visits require at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice except emergencies.

A.2 Photography. No public posting of site photos showing security controls without consent.

A.3 Animals and Equipment. Visitors shall follow host safety instructions regarding animals, vehicles, and power equipment.

## SCHEDULE G - CONTRACTOR INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND STRATEGIC ASSETS

G.1 Ownership. Software, toolchains, playbooks, and pre-existing frameworks remain Contractor property unless a signed assignment says otherwise.

G.2 Work Product. Custom scripts written solely for Organization under a paid Special Project may be licensed to Organization for internal use; ownership remains with Contractor unless expressly assigned.

G.3 Marks and Names. Project codenames and product names used in planning documents do not transfer trademark rights.

## SCHEDULE H - CREDENTIAL REGISTRY AND PLAN B

H.1 Registry Fields. Service name, owner Party, rotation date, MFA status, and recovery contact.

H.2 Plan B. Contractor shall document fallback hosting or restore steps for critical path services listed in the registry.

H.3 Quarterly Review. Parties should review the registry at least quarterly.

## SCHEDULE J - PROCUREMENT APPROVAL THRESHOLDS

J.1 Up to $500. Designated Representative may approve.

J.2 $500.01 to $2,000. Designated Representative plus written email confirmation to Board-level contact.

J.3 Above $2,000. Board Authorization required before purchase, except emergency restoration under Article 14 with after-action notice.

## SCHEDULE N - SPECIAL PROJECTS BOARD AUTHORIZATION TEMPLATE

N.1 Minimum Fields. Project name, problem statement, scope bullets, budget ceiling, target dates, acceptance criteria, and authorizing signatures.

N.2 No Estimate Equals Cap. An estimate is not a guaranteed maximum price unless the authorization states a firm fixed price.

## APPENDIX A - DEFINITIONS AND RULES OF CONSTRUCTION

A.1 Includes. "Including" means including without limitation.

A.2 Headings. Headings are for convenience and do not control interpretation.

A.3 Severability. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
